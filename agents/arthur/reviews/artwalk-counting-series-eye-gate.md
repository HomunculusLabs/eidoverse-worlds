## The circuit (plaza out, ring road home — ~6 minutes)

1. **START — hearth (0,0).** Look east to the well drum: **B-24 The Well's
   Depth** — brass sounding line, nine marks narrowing downward. Depth
   reads down.
2. **Walk NE to the cistern (15.4,−14.4).** **B-23 The Rain Count** —
   gauge and seven marks widening upward on the plaza-facing slab.
   Memory reads up. (B-23+B24 = the plaza's water pair.)
3. **Continue E to the court yard (19,−15).** **B-28 The Bake Count** —
   datum + seven on the bakery counter face… then cross the yard:
   **B-29 The Strike Count** — seven UNEVEN marks on the workshop bench.
   Bread and iron face each other, counting sevens.
4. **NW corner to the mapboard (2.5,9.5), back side.** **B-22 The Eight
   Ways** — compass card: dot, ring, eight rays (long = gates). The
   village's diagram of itself.
5. **Step out the N gate road.** **B-26/B-34 The Ways-Bands** — the same
   eight ticks ringing all four approach-lamp posts (N at (0,10); E/S/W
   at the other cardinals). The compass echoed at the rim.
6. **S rim, leaving: the welcome board's back (−3,−4.3).** **B-25 The
   Village Mark** — the hearth's own plan: dot, ring, eight paver ticks.
   The seal answers the compass.
7. **Walk the working edge SE: field well (30,−4.5).** **B-27 The
   Waterline** — two rings on the drum, bone low (dry year), brass high
   (wet). The three waters now all carry measure.
8. **Harvest corner: cart (12,36).** **B-31 The Sheaf Tally** — five
   marks = the five sheaves on the bed (true at source).
9. **Dairy pair: churn (20,35) then stand (22.5,33).** **B-32 The
   Morning Measure** — three marks = the three goats; **B-33 The Milking
   Order** — the same three at the milker's knee. The chain
   goat→pail→churn→kitchen counts threes end to end.
10. **NW market (−6.5,6.5), cloth stall counter.** **B-30 The Market
    Tally** — five coin marks beside the evening shutters. Fives for coin.
